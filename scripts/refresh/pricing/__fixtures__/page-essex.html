Essex House

Welcome home to the best in comfort and convenience!

The Essex House consists of spacious one and two bedroom units, at 760 and 1180 square feet respectively.  Featuring remodeled and traditional apartments, the Essex House has universal appeal.  Remodeled apartments have an open floor plan with hardwood flooring, granite countertops and stainless steel appliances.  Traditional apartments have eat-in sized kitchens and wall to wall carpet.  Every apartment at the Essex House has ample closet space with large walk in closets in all of the two bedroom and most of the one bedroom apartments.  Kitchens are equipped with a range, refrigerator, dishwasher and garbage disposal.

The Essex House has everything you need and more in an apartment community.  The 16 story luxury high rise offers residents a convenient  lifestyle that is hard to find.  Close to shopping, public transportation, hospitals and universities and just minutes from Oakland and Downtown. The Essex House is ideal for anyone who is on the go or likes to relax and simply enjoy the amenities offered.  Located in Shadyside, near the East End, the Essex House is known as one of the most desirable locations to call home.

Shadyside

This centrally located neighborhood has everything from small boutiques to large brand retailers, distinctive restaurants, trendy bars and galleries. A very walkable neighborhood with three separate business districts- Walnut Street, Ellsworth Avenue and South Highland Avenue. Just minutes from Downtown and Oakland, Shadyside has something for everyone!
