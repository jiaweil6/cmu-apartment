Claybourne Apartments

The Claybourne consists of large one and two-bedroom apartments, at 748 and 1184 square feet respectively. Featuring remodeled and traditional apartments, the Claybourne has everything you are looking for.  Remodeled apartments have an open floor plan with hardwood flooring, granite countertops and stainless steel appliances.  Traditional apartments have wall to wall carpeting.  Kitchens are equipped with a gas range, refrigerator, dishwasher, and garbage disposal.  Located in the heart of Shadyside, the Claybourne is just minutes from Downtown and Oakland. It is also close to shopping, public transportation, hospitals and universities. The Claybourne is a great place to call home.

Shadyside

This centrally located neighborhood has everything from small boutiques to large brand retailers, distinctive restaurants, trendy bars and galleries. A very walkable neighborhood with three separate business districts- Walnut Street, Ellsworth Avenue and South Highland Avenue. Just minutes from Downtown and Oakland, Shadyside has something for everyone!
