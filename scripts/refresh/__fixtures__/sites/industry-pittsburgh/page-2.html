01 · A Tuesday at Industry

Scroll a whole day

Not a highlight reel. A Tuesday in October.

6:45
  1. 6:45

    Fitness, before the city wakes

    A fitness center with free weights. Down the hall, not across town.

  2. 8:10

    Coffee downstairs

    Complimentary coffee bar by the clubroom. Your package is in the package room, and the management team knows your name.

  3. 8:40

    A walk to the hospital

    Out the door onto Centre Avenue. UPMC Shadyside is a walk, not a commute, and the busway at Negley is four minutes.

  4. 12:30

    Lunch on Walnut Street

    Thirteen minutes on foot. Cafe Moulin, The Urban Tap, or Noodlehead on Highland if it's a noodle kind of day.

  5. 3:00

    The afternoon from co-working

    Fiber Wi-Fi, a built-in desk upstairs, and a view that beats the office. Take the call from here.

  6. 6:15

    Courtyard, golden hour

    The outdoor kitchen and long tables, the grill going and the neighbors out. Wine optional, encouraged.

  7. 9:30

    Home, six floors up

    Big windows, loft ceilings, a full-size washer and dryer and the East End as the night light.
